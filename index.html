About Us

Simmering's has been family owned and operated since 1934

We are a family-owned upholstery business dedicated to delivering high-quality craftsmanship and personalized service.

With years of experience, we specialize in restoring and reupholstering furniture to its original beauty, blending classic techniques with contemporary styles.

Proudly serving the local community, we strive to build lasting relationships with our customers, one piece at a time.

Our Services

Four crafts, one workshop on 10th Avenue

From a single cushion to a full living room set, every piece is handled by our own team in Calgary.

No. 01

Reupholstery

Designed with you around the details and finishes you choose. We can work from your own design or a photograph, in leather or fabric from major suppliers' sample libraries. Special requests are welcome.

No. 02

Cleaning

We have a reputation for restoring and cleaning the most delicate fabrics such as tapestries and needlepoint.

No. 03

Repair

We offer a range of repair services, including but not limited to fixing legs, frames, and springs.

No. 04

Scotchgard

A spray that is applied to furniture and fabric to protect it from water and stains.
